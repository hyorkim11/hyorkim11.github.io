## Performance Metrics (Jan-May 2025)
We A/B tested this new design to approximately 50% of our app traffic for 4 months. We were able to attribute $14M in incremental revenue with this update!

## Project Brief
This project aimed to redesign the top-level information architecture of the product details page to improve overall usability and improve conversion. In addition, it required to address underlying technical issues that caused the team to not perform effeciently. My role as the Product Designer, involved conducting extensive research, developing wireframes, maintaining constant communication with engineers, and implementing design solutions that bridged the gap between aesthetics and functionality.
